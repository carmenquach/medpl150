Homework Five



Emotion Eye


For this assignment, I wanted to incorporate one of my favorite artists, NewJeans. The reference photo that I chose was a close-up of one of the members, Hanni. I wanted to recreate her doll-like eyes, and since the assignment required me to convey an emotional expression, I decided to make her look sad. This was my first time using Illustrator in a very long time, so I had to practice a lot to get the design I wanted. I used the Pen tool to create the shapes of the eye, and I used the Gradient tool to add some depth and dimension to the design. Overall, I am pretty happy with how it turned out, and I think that the design manages to showcase the aesthetic I was aiming for. It resembles both my art style along with my support for NewJeans, and overall I had a lot of fun working on this assignment.


Illustrative Artwork


My goal for this artwork was to create something that was more complex and detailed than the previous design. For my reference photos, I chose a photo from one of my favorite games, Zenless Zone Zero. In the game, they have a neo-futuristic city setting, which inspired me to create a piece with a similar vibe. I used the Pen tool to outline the shapes of the skyscrapers, shadows, and windows. Each window was individually created and warped to fit the approximate perspective of the building. I also used the Gradient tool to add some depth and dimension to the design, and I used the Adobe Color website to create a color palette that complemented the overall aesthetic I was going for. I am relatively happy with the final result, however I still think there are some areas I could have improved on. For instance, the windows could have been warped better to fit the perspective of the buildings, and I could have added more details to make the piece more visually appealing. Regardless, this was a very fun assignment and it raelly challenged me to step out of my comfort zone and learn Illustrator's features more in depth.
